Portland, Oregon

Life gets better
when you share it
with strangers.

Somewhere along the way, we stopped talking to people we didn't already know. We got busy. We got screens. We got algorithms that showed us more of what we already liked - and fewer of the people we hadn't met yet.

The result? A lot of us are lonelier than we should be in cities full of interesting people. Not because we're unfriendly. But because the structures that used to create accidental friendship - the neighborhood bar, the church hall, the front porch - they quietly disappeared.

WithStrangers is a small attempt to build those structures back. We create the conditions. You bring the courage. The rest takes care of itself.

We started with dinner because a shared meal is the oldest human technology for connection. We're adding more. Game nights. Eventually other things we haven't thought of yet. Always in Portland first. Always small before we scale. Always relational before we're anything else.

If you're lonely, you're welcome here. If you're curious, even better. If you're just brave enough to show up - that's all we ask.
